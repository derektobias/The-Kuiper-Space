About KuiperSpace

A free, interactive planetary science toolkit built for students, educators, and anyone curious about the solar system.

What is this?

KuiperSpace is an educational web application that brings real astronomical data to life through interactive tools and visualizations. Instead of reading static tables, you can compare planets side by side, simulate scenarios in different gravitational environments, watch orbits animate in real time, and explore photorealistic 3D models of every planet.

All data is sourced from NASA, JPL, and other authoritative scientific institutions, and is updated periodically to reflect the latest discoveries.

Who is this for?

Students — explore planetary science concepts in a hands-on, visual way that goes beyond textbook diagrams.

Educators — use the tools as classroom demonstrations or assign them as interactive exercises to bring astronomy lessons to life.

Enthusiasts — dig into real data, compare obscure moons, and satisfy your curiosity about how different the solar system's worlds really are.

Data Sources

Planetary data is drawn from NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), NASA Science, and peer-reviewed astronomical sources. Texture maps for the 3D Planet Viewer are sourced from Solar System Scope.

KuiperSpace is an independent educational project focused on astronomy and planetary science. Data, simulations, calculations, and visualizations may contain inaccuracies or simplified approximations.
